How It All Started

When I moved to Udaipur for 11th class, finding the right school was a difficult and confusing process.

After completing my BCA, that personal experience inspired an idea — what if students and parents could discover, compare, and apply to schools on one simple platform? Edu Portal was created to make school searching easier for everyone.
