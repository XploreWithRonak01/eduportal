Privacy Policy

How we collect, use, and protect your information

Last updated: May 24, 2026

At Edu Portal, we take your privacy seriously. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect your personal information when you use our website and services. By using Edu Portal, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

1. Information We Collect

When you use Edu Portal, we may collect the following types of information through admission forms and account registration:

  • Personal Details: Full name, date of birth, gender
  • Contact Information: Phone number, email address, residential address
  • Academic Details: Previous school, marks, class applying for
  • School Preferences: Preferred schools, board (CBSE/RBSE/ICSE), city

2. How We Use Your Information

The information we collect is used strictly for admission-related purposes and platform services:

  • Processing and submitting admission applications to selected schools
  • Sending application status updates via SMS and email
  • Communicating important admission dates and deadlines
  • Providing customer support and responding to queries
  • Improving our platform and user experience

3. Data Security & Protection

We take data security seriously. All user information is securely stored using industry-standard encryption and security measures. We implement appropriate technical and organizational safeguards to protect your personal data against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. Access to personal information is restricted to authorized personnel only.

4. We Never Sell Your Data

Edu Portal does not sell, rent, trade, or misuse your personal information. We do not share your data with third parties for marketing or advertising purposes. Your information is only shared with the schools you apply to, and solely for the purpose of processing your admission application.

5. Technical Data Collection

For website performance, security, and analytics purposes, we may automatically collect certain non-personal technical information when you visit Edu Portal:

  • Browser type and version
  • Device type (desktop, mobile, tablet)
  • Pages visited and session duration
  • IP address (anonymized) and general location

This data is used purely for analytics and security — it cannot identify you personally.

6. Third-Party Services

Our website may contain links to external websites or third-party services (such as school websites). Please note that Edu Portal is not responsible for the privacy practices of these external sites. We encourage users to review the privacy policies of any third-party websites they visit through our platform.

7. Your Rights & Control

You have full control over your personal information. As a user, you have the right to:

  • Access: Request a copy of your personal data we hold
  • Update: Correct or update any inaccurate information
  • Delete: Request removal of your personal data from our system
  • Withdraw: Withdraw consent for data processing at any time

To exercise any of these rights, simply contact us with your request.

8. Your Consent

By using Edu Portal and submitting your information through our platform, you consent to this Privacy Policy and agree to the collection, use, and storage of your data as described herein. If you do not agree with this policy, please refrain from using our services.

9. Updates to This Policy

Edu Portal reserves the right to update or modify this Privacy Policy at any time. Changes will be effective immediately upon posting on this page. We encourage users to periodically review this policy to stay informed about how we protect your information. Significant changes may be communicated via email or website notification.
